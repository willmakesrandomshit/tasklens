# Code of conduct

We want a welcoming and respectful project. Be considerate, assume good faith, and welcome constructive disagreement. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical; please do not post sensitive details publicly.
